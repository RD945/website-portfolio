BEFORE YOU MAKE IT YOURS

A few good questions.

What am I buying?

A website design and build package with the pages and features listed on its product card. These are sample offers for this storefront preview; final deliverables must be agreed before purchase.

Can I customize the design?

Yes. The packages are starting points for your own branding, text, and imagery. Extra pages, integrations, or features should be included in your brief so the scope can be confirmed.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Hosting, domain registration, paid plugins, and third-party subscriptions are separate unless explicitly included in your final agreement.

How long will my website take?

The schedule depends on the package, your content, and any custom requirements. Confirm a delivery date before placing a paid order.

Can I update the website myself?

Packages marked with an editable CMS include a content-management setup. Other packages include a source-file handover. Check each package’s details for the editing approach.

Can I pay through this website?

This storefront currently supports browsing, a saved cart, and a downloadable order brief. Payment processing and order submission are not connected. Downloading a brief does not place an order or reserve a delivery slot.

What if I need something completely custom?

Add the closest package to your cart, then describe the custom pages, features, and integrations in your order brief. They will need a separate scope and quote.
